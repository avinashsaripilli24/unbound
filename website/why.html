05Why Unbound

Spaces should spark brilliance in the people who use them.

We create serene, comfortable spaces that represent our clients and how they want to live: places that deepen connection, foster belonging and help people do well.

05.1Six reasons

  1. We are transparent

    Clear communication at every step of your building journey, with no surprises on cost or timeline.

  2. We encourage creativity

    From unique ideas to new products, our flexible team always encourages your creativity to flow.

  3. We promote collaboration

    Building a home is a team effort. Yours will be a smooth collaboration between you, our designers and our builders.

  4. One unified team

    A concept-to-completion model that brings together talent across architecture, consulting, design, engineering, technology and project operations.

  5. Attention to detail

    Strategy, design and execution are woven together, so you can make better-informed decisions quickly and with greater cost predictability.

  6. Human-centred, sustainable design

    Designed by people, for people, with materials chosen consciously and nature treated with care.

05.2What we believe

Our spaces are designed by people, for people. Let your space do the talking.

05.3Our responsibility

Conscious capitalism: people, planet and profit.

Our social responsibility framework stands on four pillars, each touching an essential part of how we work.

  1. People

    Driving positive social change where we work.

  2. Clients & suppliers

    Creating positive, sustainable projects by partnering with like-minded people.

  3. Planet

    Making sure we minimise our environmental impact.

  4. Workplaces

    Supporting sustainable spaces that inspire our people to embody our values and culture.
