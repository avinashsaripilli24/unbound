Common questions

What happens after I write to you?

We reply within a few days. Then we talk, with no obligation. After that we meet and write your design brief together.

Is the budget estimate the final price?

No. The estimate helps you decide whether to go ahead. The final price is the fixed-price contract you sign before building starts.

I already have drawings. Can you build from them?

Yes. Start from nothing or bring your own design. We work from where you are.

Why are there no past projects on this site?

On purpose. Every project starts from zero, so yours will not look like anyone else's. We would rather show you how we work.
