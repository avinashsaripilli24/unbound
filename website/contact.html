06.1Before you write

Questions people ask us first.

What happens after I write to you?

We read your note and reply within a few days. Then comes a first conversation, with no obligation, and after that a consultation meeting where we write your design brief together.

When do I pay, and for what?

At three points, each stated before it is due. A design fee when you sign the Design Agreement, covering the site survey, proof-of-concept design and a budget estimate. A plans fee of 2% of the budget estimate when you sign the Preparation of Plans Agreement, which includes council and consultant fees through the design process. A deposit when you sign the fixed-price contract.

Is the budget estimate the final price?

No. The estimate is there to help you decide whether to go ahead. The price you sign is the fixed-price contract, prepared from the drawings and specifications you select and costed room by room.

I already have drawings. Can you take it from there?

Yes. Whether you are starting from scratch or already have a design developed, we can work from where you are.

Why can't I see your past projects here?

It is deliberate. Every project we take on starts from zero, shaped by the people and the place, so yours will not look like anyone else's. We would rather show you how we think, draw and build, and answer the rest in person.

Who will I be dealing with?

One team from concept to completion: architects, engineers and kaarigars, with a single point of accountability.
