04How we work

From the first conversation to the handover.

Five stages, one unified team and a clear document at every step, so you always know where your project stands.

  1. 01Discovery
  2. 02Design
  3. 03Documentation
  4. 04Execution
  5. 05Delivery
04.1  Stage 1

Discovery

We listen, walk the site and shape a first concept.

First consultation

We meet to discuss your vision and write a detailed design brief: how you want to live, from function through to aesthetics. Bring your inspirations, images, styles, floor plans and ideas.

Site survey

We inspect and survey the site, recording its levels and features so that every complexity is defined early.

Creating the concept

Through conversations on and off site we get to know your personality, your sense of aesthetics and your lifestyle, so the concept reflects who you are.

Concept presentation

Preliminary designs and mood boards communicate the colour, texture and ambience of the project.

You receive
A design brief, site survey, concept design and budget estimate
You sign
Design Agreement

The design fee covers the site survey, proof-of-concept design and a budget estimate. The estimate is an estimate only, not the contract quotation.

04.2  Stage 2

Design

The concept becomes drawings, renders and numbers.

Detailed layout

Drawings and renders so you can visualise your plan, with a detailed bill of quantities for materials.

Detailed design

Every aspect of the scheme is detailed, specified and proposed for your approval.

Proof of concept & budget estimate

A scaled floor plan, front elevation and 3D render, revised until it suits you. Then a personalised budget estimate, for transparency and peace of mind.

You receive
Drawings, renders, a bill of quantities and a personalised budget estimate
04.3  Stage 3

Documentation

Everything is specified, priced and agreed before work begins.

Preparation of Plans Agreement

Once you are confident in the concept and the budget estimate, we prepare the PPA. Fees at this stage equal 2% of the budget estimate, and include all council and consultant fees through the design process.

Development drawings

All the designs and drawings needed for execution, and for your own use afterwards.

Materials & specification

You choose appliances, fixtures, floor coverings, colour palettes and finishes, with our team guiding every selection.

Fixed-price contract

A detailed cost plan shows the cost of each element, room by room. Custom furniture and bought-out items are listed individually with vendor options, and the timeline and delivery schedule are set before execution.

Sign-off

With your approval, the documents are signed and the deposit is paid. We are ready to build.

You receive
Development drawings, specifications and a room-by-room cost plan
You sign
Preparation of Plans Agreement and the fixed-price contract
04.4  Stage 4

Execution

We build, on site and in the workshop, with expert supervision and quality checks throughout.

Building it

The application is lodged and work begins. Every issue is resolved promptly and efficiently, to your satisfaction.

You receive
Supervised execution, and a team that stays in touch at every step
04.5  Stage 5

Delivery

Dressing and handover.

Dressing & handover

Once construction and carpentry are complete, we deliver and position every furnishing. We curate artwork and accessories, or use your existing treasures, to give each space your own signature.

You receive
Your space, dressed and ready to live in
