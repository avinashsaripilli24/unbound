How it works

Five steps. You always know which one comes next.

  1. 1

    Listen

    We meet, walk your site and understand how you want to live.

    You get A written brief, a site survey, a first concept and a budget estimate.

    More detail

    Bring photos, plans and ideas you like. We survey the site, then show you a concept with mood boards. You sign a Design Agreement, and its fee covers the survey, the concept and the estimate. The estimate is a guide, not the final price.

  2. 2

    Design

    The concept becomes drawings, 3D views and numbers.

    You get Drawings, 3D views, a list of materials and a budget made for you.

    More detail

    We detail every part of the design. The floor plan, front view and 3D view are revised until they suit you. The list of materials and quantities is called a bill of quantities (BoQ).

  3. 3

    Agree

    Everything is chosen, priced and signed before work begins.

    You get Final drawings, your chosen materials and a fixed-price contract.

    More detail

    First you sign a Preparation of Plans Agreement (PPA). You then choose fixtures, floors, colours and finishes with our team. The contract shows the cost of every room, with vendor options, a timeline and a delivery schedule.

  4. 4

    Build

    We build it, on site and in our workshop.

    You get Expert supervision, quality checks and a team that stays in touch.

  5. 5

    Hand over

    We furnish it, style it and hand it over.

    You get Your space, ready to live in.

    More detail

    We deliver and place every furnishing. We choose art and accessories, or use pieces you already love.

What you pay, and when

Three payments before building starts. Each one is told to you in advance.

  1. Design fee

    When you sign the Design Agreement. It covers the site survey, the concept and the budget estimate.

  2. Plans fee: 2% of the budget estimate

    When you sign the plans agreement. It includes council and consultant fees.

  3. Deposit

    When you sign the fixed-price contract.
