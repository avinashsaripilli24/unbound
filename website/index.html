Don't expect to see your dream home on our site.

It hasn't been built yet. Every project we take on starts from zero, shaped by the people who will live there and by the place itself. So we show you how we think, draw and build, and let that earn your trust.

01.4In writing

What we put in writing.

Trust is easier with paper. Before anything is built, these are agreed and signed.

  1. The first meeting carries no obligation.

    We begin every job by getting a feeling for what you need, on site and off it.

  2. You see a budget estimate before you commit to plans.

    It comes with the concept design, so you decide with numbers in hand.

  3. Every fee is stated before it is due.

    The design fee, the plans fee and the deposit each have their own agreement.

  4. The contract is fixed-price.

    It is prepared from the drawings and specifications you select, with a cost plan for each room and vendor options for furniture and bought-out items.

  5. The timeline is set before work begins.

    So is the delivery schedule.

  6. One team answers for the whole project.

    Architects, engineers and kaarigars, from concept to handover.
