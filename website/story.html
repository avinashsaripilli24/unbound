02Our story

Something meant to be.

The genesis of something meant to be is always laced with grit, a looming sense of uncertainty, innumerable questions and, through it all, a prevailing sense of passion. The story of Studio Unbound is no exception.

02.1Who we are

Architects, artisans, builders and engineers.

We are a team based in Visakhapatnam, practising an architecture that stays close to people, to the environment and to the resources of a place.

We keep pushing boundaries to give spaces a new definition and everyday living a new radiance. It takes us back to our primal roots, and it leaves behind objects that speak for themselves and bring a space together.

02.2Design as art

We show the thinking and the making.

Where a conventional studio shows finished rooms, we show the sketch that began them and the hands that made them. The story behind a space matters as much as the space itself.

Before there is a room, there is a mark on paper. The play of solid and void, the placement of a window, the way light meets a wall. This is where the design is decided, and we treat it as art.

Our founder prefers to let the experience do the talking.

By our work shall you know us.

02.4The workshop

Workmanship, in progress.
