Our story

Studio Unbound began the way most good things begin: with grit, many questions and a lot of passion.

We design spaces that stay close to people, to nature and to the place they stand in. Every project starts from zero, so no two look alike.

What we believe

  • Spaces are for people.

    Designed by people, for people. Your space should do the talking.

  • Be open about everything.

    Clear communication at every step, on cost and on time.

  • Respect the earth.

    We choose materials with care and treat nature with care.
